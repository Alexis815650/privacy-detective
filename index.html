CASE 01 — ENCRYPTION KEY

Someone locked the evidence.

The app's evidence file is encrypted. You need to discover the five-letter key before you can inspect it.

?
DECRYPTION CLUE

A secure app should earn this from its users. It is easy to lose, difficult to rebuild, and essential when people share private information. What is the five-letter word?

Enter a five-letter guess to begin.

How the key was discovered

The computer checks your guess against the secret word one letter at a time. Green means a letter is in the correct position, yellow means the letter exists elsewhere in the word, and gray means the letter is not available in the answer (or has already been accounted for).

This is a logic puzzle inspired by password and encryption concepts. Real encryption uses mathematical algorithms and cryptographic keys; it is not normally broken by guessing a word from a clue.
